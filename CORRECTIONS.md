# Corrections

## 2026-10-27 — errata to knese-symmetric-multiaffine-agler 1.1.0-candidate (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23283382)

The archived PDF, the manuscript source, the Zenodo deposit and the tag `v1.1.0-candidate` are unchanged. These errata were confirmed by the Evidence Press archive audit and are also shown at <https://evidencepress.org/releases/knese-symmetric-multiaffine-agler/>.

1. Corrected the hypothesis of Lemma 4. The archived text says q has “no zeros in D”, the open disk. The proof needs no zeros in the closed disk D̄; with only the open disk the lemma fails, for example for q = 1 − t, n = 1. Lemma 4 should read “Let q have degree at most n and have no zeros in the closed disk D̄”, and in the proof in §6, q_R(t) = q(Rt) should be said to have no zeros in D̄.

   The proof already needs the closed-disk condition, so the hypothesis is corrected to match it. The audit found no stated result that relies on the weaker open-disk wording.
