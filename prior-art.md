# Primary comparisons and bounded novelty evidence

Inspected and refreshed on 10 October 2026. The claimed residual theorem is automatic Schur-Agler membership for the exact reflected quotient of every stable symmetric multiaffine polynomial, in every finite variable count. The new candidate mechanism is the weighted permutation-average identity, its equal-diagonal polarization, and the resulting positive unit-root Gram formula. Finite Blaschke interpolation and general Agler consequences are inherited machinery.
